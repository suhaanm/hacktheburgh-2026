Design system, version 0.2, 8 Nov 2026

Quiet by default. Specific when it speaks.

Knack is a calm instrument for people doing real work. It shows where time is lost, helps on the real screen, then steps back as the person improves. This page documents how it looks, how it moves, and what every mark means.

  • One idea per screenOne hero visual or one task. Everything else waits behind a click.
  • Every mark means somethingSize, colour, opacity and motion each encode one thing, and a legend says which.
  • Help fades, so the interface fadesThe coach shrinks, becomes a dot, then leaves. A returning worker sees it come back.

What the ring does

  • Eases onto the target in 560 ms and overshoots nothing.
  • Carries one line of text, in the coach's voice, never a paragraph.
  • Hides within 240 ms when the page scrolls or the DOM changes, then lands again once the element is found.
  • Reduced motion: the ring appears in place.

Practice

Rules for new components

Every new component follows these ten. Break one only with a reason written in docs/DECISIONS.md.

  1. One hero per screen.Everything else sits behind a click.
  2. One short helper line under a title.One line at 1440 px, about 90 characters.
  3. Details in a disclosure or a Hint.Disclosure first; Hint for a unit, a definition or an interval.
  4. At most 7 rows in a default list.Then "Show all N".
  5. Numbers carry their unit and a badge.Interval and n sit behind a Hint on worker screens; admin and Evidence screens print them.
  6. No walls of text.No paragraph over two lines of 65 characters in a default view.
  7. No gradients, glows, emoji or accent bars.Hatching and range bands are data, not decoration.
  8. Few controls.One primary action; at most two quiet ones; row actions on hover or focus, always on touch.
  9. A Hint never holds the only copy of a fact.Not a limitation, a consequence, an error or an origin.
  10. Look at it.Light and dark, 1440 and 390 px, keyboard focus, reduced motion.

Practice

Do and don't

Do

  • Say what will happen."Try in shadow mode. Nothing is submitted." Then show the result in the same words.
  • Make "Not now" and "Never for this" as easy as "Yes".Same size, same row, no guilt.
  • Keep n and the interval one click away.Behind a Hint or "About this data" for workers; printed for admins and on Evidence. Badge synthetic data every time.
  • Let one thing be the hero.One visual or one task. Count the controls on the first view and remove any that are not needed.
  • Use a side panel or inline expansion.The worker keeps their place.
  • Defer secondary detail to a Hint.One or two sentences: a unit, a definition, an interval. Never the only copy of a fact.

Don't

  • Use colour alone for state.Pair it with text, shape or a tick pattern.
  • Decorate.No gradients, glows, sparkles, emoji section markers or accent bars on cards.
  • Hide consent.No pre-ticked boxes, no buried "never", no modal after modal.
  • Draw a chart without a scale.No pies, no 3D, no unlabelled axes, no dual axes.
  • Loop an animation for atmosphere.If it does not show a change, remove it.
  • Use the title attribute or a hover-only popover.Use a Hint (works on focus and tap) or a disclosure.
  • Print a wall of text.No paragraph over two lines of 65 characters in a default view.
