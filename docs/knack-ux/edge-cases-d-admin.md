# Edge cases: the Admin screens

Every state that can break an Admin screen, what a person sees instead, and the test that proves it. The screens are Firm, Roles, People, Onboarding, Automation, Risk and coverage, Forecasts and the Firm brief. The rule for all of them: a state is said in words, never a blank page, and never "NaN", "undefined", "null" or "Infinity" on screen. Tests render the real components in jsdom on hostile data and read the text a person would read (`src/components/firm/edge-cases.test.ts`); the sentences and the confidence word are pure and tested in `insights.test.ts` and `forecastInsights.test.ts`.

The words on these screens are fixed templates filled by code from the answers the Admin routes already give. A model never writes a sentence an admin reads, so there is no edge case where a model says something the data does not.

Test names below are `describe > it` in `edge-cases.test.ts` unless a file is named.

| State | What is shown | Test |
|---|---|---|
| Empty firm: no people, no roles, no insights, no repeated work | each screen opens with one plain sentence: "No roles to compare yet.", "No one is listed yet.", "No one has been followed through onboarding yet.", "No repeated work stands out across 0 people yet.", "No risk figures have been sent yet.", "No one is in the firm view yet." No chart, no table row, no Show all | `empty > empty firm says so in words on every screen`, `empty > empty firm gives no forecast cards and no charts` |
| Empty forecasts | "Nothing to forecast yet. Forecasts appear once people have been followed for weeks." The reliability row stays | `forecastInsights.test.ts > nothing in, nothing out` |
| One person | "Only 1 person so far, too few for a trend." The people list shows the person; the group figure is replaced by "Fewer than 5 people with data in this group". No forecast card | `one person > one person is one person, with no group figure`, `one person > one person gives no forecast` |
| A group under the minimum (fewer than 5) | Roles: "No role has 5 experts and 5 juniors yet, so none is compared." and the row says "Not compared. Needs 5 experts and 5 juniors; this role has 3 and 9." Onboarding: a role under 5 people is left out of the chart and the sentence and the table says "not shown". Forecasts: repeated work by fewer than 5 people is "Too early to say" | `a group under the minimum > …` (three tests) |
| 10 000 occurrences, or 10 000 people | counts print with a thousands separator ("10,000 people", "1,000,000" times); the sentence stays one line; a 2 000-person role is still one headline | `10 000 occurrences > …` (two tests), `insights.test.ts > 10 000 occurrences print with a thousands separator` |
| An interval that spans zero | Roles: the dot is hollow and the sentence says "No gap between juniors and experts can be read yet." Onboarding and Forecasts: the sentence says "may ... but the gap is not clear yet" and the confidence word is "Too early to say". Never a direction the interval does not support | `an interval that spans zero > …` (two tests), `insights.test.ts > an interval that spans zero is still a readable sentence`, `forecastInsights.test.ts > overlapping intervals are said in words` |
| Two medians that overlap, or one with no interval | Onboarding headline: "Data analysts may reach the target sooner than benefits processors, but the gap is not clear yet." The chart still draws both dots; the one without an interval says "(no interval yet)" | `insights.test.ts > onboarding says the gap is not clear while one median has no interval`, `forecastInsights.test.ts > a median with no interval is too early to say` |
| NaN or null fields | a NaN or null number is read as missing: the row says "not enough data" or is left out of the chart and the table; a null name becomes "this role" or the person's label; no sentence contains a bad token | `NaN and null fields > …` (four tests), `insights.test.ts > NaN and null fields never reach a sentence`, `forecastInsights.test.ts > NaN and null in the wire never reach a card` |
| A very long role or person name | sentences and chart labels cut it at a word with an ellipsis (32 characters in a sentence, 22 in a chart label); the table cell keeps the whole name and wraps it (`overflow-wrap: anywhere`), so a 200-character name stays in its cell | `very long and unicode names > …`, `insights.test.ts > keeps a very long name to one line`, `a name cut for length ends at a word` |
| Unicode and emoji names | counted by characters, never split; a name in another script or ending in a symbol takes no English plural "s" ("数据分析师 reach the target ...") | `very long and unicode names > unicode and emoji names are never split into broken characters`, `insights.test.ts > makes a role name plural for a sentence` |
| Numbers from years ago | one line under the headline: "These numbers are from 1 March 2022, 4 years ago." | `numbers dated in the future or years ago > numbers from years ago say so in words`, `insights.test.ts > says so in words when they are weeks, months or years old` |
| Numbers dated after today | "These numbers are dated 1 January 2027, which is after today. Check the clock." Example data never shows this line: an example's date is not a claim, and the example note already says what it is | `numbers dated in the future or years ago > numbers dated after today say so in words`, `insights.test.ts > says so when they are dated after today` |
| A date nobody can read, or a broken clock | no freshness line; a forecast drops the month ("through December") and keeps the sentence | `numbers dated ... > numbers from yesterday, and a date nobody can read, say nothing`, `a broken clock still gives a forecast, without a month` |
| Negative gains: juniors ahead of experts, a negative median or hours | the sentence says "more", not a minus sign ("12 points more of their steps without help than experts"); the axis holds the positive side; a negative or zero hours row is left out of the chart | `negative gains > …` (two tests), `forecastInsights.test.ts > a negative gain (juniors ahead of experts) says more, not fewer` |
| A role with nothing to count (no steps, no skills, no habits, no curve) | a person with no per-skill figures opens to "No per-skill figures yet."; a role with no curve says "No curve yet" and why; a role with no habits still gets a "Do" line ("Look at the steps where juniors ask for help most.") | `a role with nothing to count (no cogs) > …` (three tests) |
| Stale readiness: nobody has enough data for a trend | People: "No one has a clear trend yet among 24 people."; Risk: "People with enough data: 0 of 24" and "Not seen lately: 24"; Forecasts: no trend card | `stale readiness > nobody has enough data for a trend`, `a card with no interval still prints n and says too early` |
| An approval backlog of one, or only stops | Risk headline names what exists and counts the rest ("1 approval is waiting, the oldest for 5 hours; 1 more thing needs a look.") | `insights.test.ts > risk lists what is waiting and what stopped` |
| The engine is down, or answers with nothing | the screen shows the example data with its note, or a calm error with Try again; the Forecasts screen reads four answers and says "Nothing to forecast yet" when none can carry a sentence | `forecastInsights.test.ts > nothing in, nothing out` (the loaders are covered by the live check, not by a unit test) |

## Rules these tests hold

- A sentence states a direction only when the interval supports it. Otherwise it says "may", "not clear yet" or "too early to say", and the confidence word is "Too early to say".
- Confidence is chosen by code: n under 5, no interval, or an interval that includes no change is "Too early to say"; n of 10 or more with a clear, not wide interval is "Likely"; the rest is "Possible".
- Forecast sentences name roles, habits and counts, never a person (`forecastInsights.test.ts > names roles, habits and counts, never a person`).
- Groups under 5 people are never compared and never drawn.
- n and the interval stay printed: n on every card and row, the interval in the chart line, the headline's detail line or the table.
