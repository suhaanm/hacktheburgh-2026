Annotations:

- Title stays the engine's sentence (it is already a hero). No eyebrow, no lede paragraph; the one printed line is the limit ("Measures help needed, not expertise."), kept as text because a limit is never hidden (D45 rule 9).
- One chart. The fade strip (help level and cue rings), the legend, the readout of every point (hover and focus keep it), and the six-line legend leave the default view: the legend becomes "Key" inside About this data; the fade becomes a closed disclosure "The fade".
- n, the interval level and the window (20 occurrences) are in About this data and in one Hint beside "85%" (worker screen: D45). The origin badge is visible.
- Skills are rows, not cards: a trend arrow, three words, and a disclosure for "keeps it after 3 days" (the spaced-recall figure). Selecting a skill filters the curve (existing behaviour kept).
- Ledger, export and delete move to Your data (one place to see and delete what is held about me); here "Your ledger" is a link to it.
- "Work done for you" keeps the rule that overnight work never moves the score; it is one line inside "What Knack did for you".
