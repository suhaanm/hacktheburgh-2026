# Edge cases of Hey Knack

State, what Knack does, and the test that pins it. "Offline" means a unit test with fakes (no microphone, no recogniser, no real input). "Needs the Mac" means it can only be seen with the real helper on the owner's machine: it is written down, not tested here.

| # | Situation | What happens | Test |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Microphone switched off in System Settings while listening | macOS ends the helper. It is started again once; the new helper is refused and exits with `mic_denied`, which is final: no more restarts, one line in the menu bar ("Hey Knack needs the microphone. Turn it on in System Settings."). After the fix, switching the listener off and on starts it | offline: helper.test "the microphone revoked while it listens"; controller.test "the microphone revoked in System Settings"; needs the Mac for the real kill |
| 2 | Microphone revoked in the middle of a conversation | the conversation's own microphone fails (`mic_unavailable`); the conversation ends; one card ("Live voice needs the microphone. Turn it on in System Settings." with a System Settings button) and the menu line; the listener comes back after its pause (and is then refused with the same code if it is still off) | offline: controller.test "the microphone going away mid-conversation"; session tests for `mic_unavailable` |
| 3 | The Dictation language is not installed (no on-device recogniser) | the helper exits with `on_device_unavailable` (or `locale_unsupported`), is not restarted, and the line says "This Mac cannot recognise speech on the device, so Hey Knack stays off." It never falls back to a server | offline: controller.test "a Dictation language that is not installed"; texts; the on-device-only request flag is in main.swift (needs the Mac) |
| 4 | Two displays | the card appears on the display that holds the pointer (bottom-right of its work area, 16 px in); the screen picture for a conversation is the primary display (as for every Knack glance): a known limit | offline: card-ipc.test "where the card goes" |
| 5 | The lid is closed and reopened, or the Mac sleeps | the lock event and the sleep event stop the listener at once; after the Mac wakes it waits 2.5 s for the audio devices, then starts again; a conversation open across a sleep ends (`connection_lost`) | offline: controller.test "the lid closed and reopened"; guards.test; needs the Mac for the real events |
| 6 | A Bluetooth headset connects or disconnects (the audio route changes) | the helper restarts its audio engine on the route-change notification; if that fails it exits with `audio_engine_failed` and the supervisor starts it again after a growing pause (1, 2, 4, 8, 15, 30 s). During a conversation the renderer's own microphone follows the route | offline: helper.test "a Bluetooth headset or the lid"; needs the Mac for the real notification |
| 7 | The helper crashes again and again | more than 5 unexpected exits in 10 minutes ends the restarts (`crash_loop`); one line says it stopped; switching the listener off and on clears it | offline: helper.test "gives up on a crash loop"; controller.test "a helper that keeps crashing" |
| 8 | The helper prints anything but its three closed lines (a transcript, a path) | the line is dropped unread, the helper is stopped and blocked (`protocol`); the words are never accepted from it | offline: helper.test "refuses ... extra_fields", "stops the helper on any line that is not one of ours" |
| 9 | "Hey Knack" during a call, a meeting or a screen share | the listener is not running then, so nothing is heard. Words that arrive in the instant a call begins are ignored with no card | offline: guards.test; controller.test "the words heard in the instant a call..." |
| 10 | "Hey Knack" while an agent acts for the worker | not listening while Knack works; the same instant-window rule; the kill hotkey is the way to stop an agent | offline: same test |
| 11 | "Hey Knack, hey Knack" | one wake: the second phrase is in the 8 s cooldown, the helper resets after the first, and the controller ignores a wake that arrives while one is being handled | offline: matching.test "the same words twice"; controller.test "the same words are not accepted twice" |
| 12 | A very long silence after the words | the conversation ends after about 30 s with no sound from the worker and no model speech (never while the model is speaking), after 5 minutes at the latest; the microphone is released with it and the listener returns after 1.5 s | offline: wake-session.test "how a hands-free conversation ends" |
| 13 | The worker keeps talking for ages | the 5 minute cap ends it; a spending limit ends it sooner | offline: wake-session.test "the five-minute cap" |
| 14 | The words are heard but a conversation cannot open (example data, no key, the service not answering, the daily limit, the microphone not allowed, Knack paused or stopped) | never silence: a card, "Hey Knack heard you." with the reason in plain words; "Open Settings" or "System Settings" opens the right place on a real click; no consent is asked for something that could not happen | offline: controller.test "the words always get an answer" |
| 15 | The first card is closed, times out or is answered with a key | nothing is remembered and no conversation opens; the next wake asks again | offline: controller.test "a key or a closed card answers nothing" |
| 16 | Stop everything while a card is up or a click is running | the card closes, the run is stopped, every wake and tool call is refused until Resume | offline: click-machine.test "kill"; controller.test "kill stops a run" |
| 17 | The model asks to click and the first supervised test has not been done | `first_supervised_test_pending`; Knack says "I will show you instead" and keeps pointing | offline: click-machine.test, controller.test |
| 18 | A tool reply is ready before the conversation's outgoing channel exists | it waits (at most 8) and goes out first, in order, when the channel opens; dropped with a session that ends first | offline: session.test "a tool call that finishes before the outgoing channel exists" |
| 19 | The same words said by someone else in the room, or by a video | the listener cannot tell who spoke; the first-wake cards and the gates are the protection, and nothing leaves the Mac without them. The recogniser's false triggers per hour are not measured | not testable offline |
| 20 | The helper binary is missing | the app starts normally; the menu bar says "Hey Knack is not installed in this build." | offline: helper.test "does not start without a binary" |
