# Video beats: 25 claims with their proof shots

For the launch teaser, the demo and the technical cut. Each beat is one line a narrator can say, paired with the shot that proves it. The tag says which cut it suits: **teaser** (about 30 seconds; the most visual), **demo** (the three-minute walk-through; labels and routes checked against the dashboard source, but rehearse once before trusting a label), **technical** (the cut for judges who read numbers).

Rules for every beat: say 'synthetic' or 'simulated' where the data is; never say novices become experts, that coaching is proven, or that nothing leaves the laptop (see 'Things we do not claim' in `docs/PITCH_FEATURES.md`). Where a beat says 'film only if', the feature is built and tested but its end-to-end behaviour has not been seen by the lead on the real desktop at the time of writing. The recording itself is owner-initiated only (decision D54).

## Before anybody records

From `docs/KNACK_DEMO.md` (on main), which holds the full shot list, spoken lines and fallbacks (scenes M1 to M16). The points that change what the camera sees:

- Launch Knack with `ALONGSIDE_OVERLAY_CAPTURABLE=1`. Without it the ring, the ghost cursor, the corner pill, the corner cards, the Cue panel and the Hey Knack cards are excluded from screen recordings (the app sets content protection on each of them), and the video shows a calm window and no coach.
- The ring is not drawn while the Knack window is focused: keep the worker's window in front for the coach and Cue shots.
- The agent's three browser windows appear on screen only if the stack was started headed (`UNDERSTUDY_AGENT_HEADED=1`).
- The coach and Cue shots can be played by a script (`apps/desktop/scripts/demo-worker.mjs`) acting as a teammate on the sandbox, with no operating-system input. Put 'played by a script on our sandbox' on screen.
- The two Hey Knack consent cards and the approval in beat 10 can only be answered by a real mouse click. Do not approve the seeded CN-2041 on camera if it is wanted for the teaser.
- At the time of writing a routine run in the agent's browser does not minimise the window by itself (press Command-M; gap G3 in `docs/KNACK_DEMO.md`). Approving a live agent item now submits the sandbox note (decision D61, which fixed gap G11); a seeded item such as CN-2041 only marks approved.
- Navigation changed on main at 02:05 UTC under the lead's views rule (`docs/knack-ux/views.md`, `apps/dashboard/src/routeRedirects.ts`): a worker has Home, Needs you, Routines, Growth and Your data; Where time goes, Your hours, How work connects, Calendar and Git habits are Growth sections; Evidence, Forecasts, Models and cost and Background runs are Admin pages. `docs/KNACK_DEMO.md` was written before this, so re-check every shot's path against the build you film, and film Evidence, Forecasts and the firm screens in the Admin view.
- The Admin view is meant to be filled with made-up people (owner request 4.4) and, under D57, the app will not say 'synthetic'. Say aloud that the firm and the people are made up. Request 4.4: the firm seed (`python -m understudy_engine.demo seed-firm`, 32 made-up people in 3 teams) is on main, but the live engine reads this device's own ledger (one person) until it has run, so shoot the multi-person Admin screens only after the seed has run on the live data.
- Recording is owner-initiated only (decision D54); the lead runs `scripts/record-demo.sh` when the owner says begin.
