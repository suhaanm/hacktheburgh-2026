Understanding the earnings taper

Once a household earns more than its work allowance, the monthly award is reduced by 55 pence for every extra pound of take-home pay. This is called the taper rate.

The work allowance is higher for households with children or a person with limited capability for work, and lower when the household also receives help with housing costs.
