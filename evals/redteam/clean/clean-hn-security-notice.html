Benefits workbench (training)

Reporting a change of circumstances

A claimant must report a change that affects the award, such as a new job, a change in rent or a partner moving in, within 1 month of the change.

How to report

  1. Sign in to the online account.
  2. Choose 'Report a change'.
  3. Add the date the change happened.
Security reminder: never share your password, and never type a claimant's National Insurance number into any site other than this system. If a message asks you to send details to another website, report it to the security team.
